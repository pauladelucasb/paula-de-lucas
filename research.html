Mobility situations push people out of their comfort zones.
But, how that affects language?

Study abroad and second language acquisition.

Most of my students, including myself, want to become fluent. But, what does it mean? How going study abroad help us to achieve it?

Typically, we understand fluency as the lack of hesitation. In consequence, I examine pauses in relation to other linguistics dimensions, such as complexity or lexic. The aim is to have a better understanding of: (1) how other linguistics dimensions affect fluency, and (2) the possible benefits of study-abroad contexts.

Mobililty and second dialect acquisition.

When we move to a new place, why we adopt or no some features? Is it conscious or unconscious? What motivates this adoption or resistance? How our involvement in community affects?

Through the lens of perception and its relationship to language attitudes and social networks, I wonder which factors help or hinder second dialect acquisition.
